A tiny world that keeps becoming.

Pip is an autonomous game creature. Its decision system weighs hunger, energy, happiness and curiosity. It remembers explored places, plants berries, gathers wood, builds a home and grows from sprout to elder. This is local game AI, not a chatbot or a conscious being.

Just watch, or lend a hand. Click the ground to leave a berry. Say hello for a little happiness boost. A world day is four simulation minutes; growing up takes about 30 simulation minutes. Pip never dies from neglect.

When you leave: reopening simulates up to eight hours at normal speed. A closed file does not actually run in the background. Pause also pauses this catch-up. Browser background throttling can slow live animation.

Keep your creature: autosave uses this browser’s local storage. Export a save for backups or to move between your PC and a website. Local-file storage varies by browser; moving the HTML or clearing browser data may lose its local save.

Portable & GitHub-ready: this entire world is this one HTML file. Open it in your browser, or put it in your GitHub Pages repository as index.html. It needs no installation, network, server, dependencies or API key. Each visitor has their own world.
